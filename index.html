1

Prepare your poster

Use a one-slide PowerPoint poster. Download the APHA PowerPoint template below. Its poster size is 36 inches wide × 21 inches high (landscape). You can also use an existing poster with the same proportions.

↓ Download APHA PowerPoint template (36 × 21 inches)
Export the poster as PNG
In PowerPoint for Windows: select File → Export → Change File Type → PNG Portable Network Graphics Format → Save As. When prompted, select Just This One or Current Slide Only. Keep both the .pptx and the PNG.

Your files are processed in this browser. Review all extracted text, charts, and sections before sharing the finished poster.

Select your files

Choose the PowerPoint and the PNG exported from that same slide.

Poster title from PowerPointUpload the completed PowerPoint to detect its title.

The tool reads the title from the first slide of the PowerPoint. It is included in the generated poster site and preparation record. Submitter name and work email are not collected.

3

Select and review sections

On the poster, drag a rectangle around each section. Include the section heading, text and any charts you want visitors to see. Then select each numbered area to review its text.

Tip: For a wide results area, create one selection for the full area or make smaller sections for individual charts. Text inside images or grouped objects may need to be typed or pasted into the editor.

Editing section

Charts remain visible in the selected poster image. Enter chart descriptions or values in the text above so they can be read aloud.

4

Add presenters

Add presenters in the order you want them to appear. Photos and email addresses are optional. The site uses the selected APHA poster template and rotating author carousel.

5

Preview and revise

The generated poster uses the same visual template and visitor tools as the Community-Informed Autism Needs Assessment site: cinematic intro, 3D exploration, full-poster view, rotating author carousel, accessible section explorer, reading controls, and narration. Preview the output, return to earlier steps to revise, then refresh the preview before downloading.

Download the HTML file and preparation record for safekeeping. Share both with Ángel Montes, or another designated staff member, for review and web publishing. The preparation time uses this device's clock; staff will record the official receipt and publication dates.

6

Request review and publishing

Share the downloaded HTML and preparation record with Ángel Montes or another designated staff member through the department's designated submission channel. Staff will review the files, publish the poster online, and provide you with its public link and a QR code. Staff do not publish directly to the repository.

Control point: This website prepares files in your browser. It does not transmit or store files, collect submitter names or emails, maintain an official audit log, or publish online by itself. The repository remains under administrator control. Do not include confidential or personally sensitive information in a poster intended for public viewing.

Generate a downloadable QR code

After staff send the published poster link and QR code, use the QR provided for your final PowerPoint. If you need another copy, paste the public link here to generate and download a QR code PNG. Place the QR near the top-right of the final poster so attendees can scan the physical poster to open its digital version.

Use the final public URL from staff. The downloadable QR is a 2048 × 2048 pixel PNG generated in this browser for print use.

Scan the code to open the published poster.
